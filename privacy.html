Threadly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Threadly is a free app for communities, posts, votes and comments. It has no ads and no trackers.

Demo mode (the default)

Until a server is connected, everything you do (your account, posts, comments, votes) is saved only on your own device, in the app's own storage. We never receive it. Clearing the app data, or pressing “Reset demo data” in Settings, deletes it.

Online mode

If the person who runs this copy of Threadly connects it to a server, then what you post is stored on that server so other people can see it. This includes your username, a password (stored in scrambled form by the server's login system, never in readable form), your posts, pictures, comments, votes, saved posts and reports. Usernames, posts and comments are visible to everyone who uses that copy of the app. The person who runs the server can see and delete this data. Do not share personal details such as your real name, address, phone number or school in a post.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. (The camera or photo picker opens only if you choose to attach a picture to a post.)
  • We do not show advertising or use tracking.

Safety

There is a family-friendly word filter (on by default). You can report any post or comment. Community creators can delete posts and comments in their community, and authors can delete their own. Threadly is meant for people aged 13 and over, or younger people using it with a parent's permission.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own posts and comments at any time. To have an account removed from an online server, contact the person who runs it, or the email below.
